03 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELS

A bit about me.

Hi, I’m Anna. A product designer with an engineer’s mind and an artist’s soul.

I like the messy middle, where complex workflows meet real people. My job is to untangle them and turn them into experiences that feel simple, and sometimes even a little joyful.

Most recently, at Miro, I designed how the canvas connects to the tools teams already use, and helped companies make Miro their own. I like owning the whole journey: research, concepts, specs, QA and launch. These days I often prototype straight in code.

Before that, I worked across startups, enterprise and independent projects, from design tools to traffic data.

Away from the screen: running shoes, a camera, time with my children and a cat with strong opinions.

04 / PINNED TO MY WALL

A few things I come back to.

01

System thinking.

Every screen is part of something bigger. I design the patterns, not just the page, so the next feature fits right in.

02

Emotional design.

People remember how a product made them feel. A little warmth and delight can turn a tool into something they actually enjoy.

03

Don’t make me think.

The best interface is the one you don’t notice. If it needs explaining, it isn’t done yet.
